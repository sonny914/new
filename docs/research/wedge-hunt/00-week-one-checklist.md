### Check 1 result (2026-10-06): YELLOW

**Document read.** https://www.enverus.com/terms-conditions/ (no "last updated" date shown; copyright notice 2014 in body, 2026 in footer). This is the Enverus website terms of use plus a standard "Access / Restrictions / Definitions" block whose own definitions refer to "this trial of Courthouse 3.0". It is Enverus's template restrictions, not the OpenInvoice supplier agreement. The supplier agreement has still not been read.

**Clauses, verbatim.**

- Third parties and outsourcing (Restrictions a): "Customer shall not and shall not permit or authorize any third party to: a. Use the Products, Services, Proprietary Data, or any Enverus API other than as authorized or sell, lease, license, sublicense, rent, loan, share, pledge, or otherwise transfer, with or without consideration, all or any part of the Products, Services, or Proprietary Data or permit third parties or Customer personnel that are not Users to benefit from them, including a timesharing, rental, outsourcing, service bureau, networking, hosted service, or other arrangement."
- Building tools (Restrictions b): "Use the Products, Services, Proprietary Data, or any Enverus API to compete with Enverus or build, develop, or provide any third parties or Customer personnel that are not Users with any software, code, scripts, models, training data, apps, platforms, exchanges, websites, widgets, plugins or other tools..."
- Automation (Restrictions c): "...use any robot, spider, or other automated method (other than an Enverus API) to access, download, or reproduce Proprietary Data..." and, for the website, "Use any robot, spider, scraper or other automated means to access the Site."
- Proprietary Data (Definitions c): "all data, databases, elements, records, documents... to which Customer has access using the Products..."
- Confidentiality (Restrictions d): "Disclose or permit any third party to use confidential information of Enverus, including (i) Proprietary Data..."
- Credentials: no explicit ban on sharing; the indemnity covers "the use of this Site by any person using your user name and/or password".
- Enforcement: "Enverus may suspend or terminate access to the Products if Customer or any User is suspected of violating these restrictions."
- "Users" is not defined in this document.

**Reading.** The manual pilot is permissible only if QB personnel can be designated as Users on the supplier's own account; otherwise "outsourcing" and "service bureau" describe QB exactly. Any software must go through an Enverus API; screen automation is barred. Building a tool for other suppliers on top of OpenInvoice data is barred without an Enverus partner arrangement.

**Open question that decides the colour.** The definition of "User" in the OpenInvoice supplier agreement. Two ways to close it: (1) ask every supplier on a call to forward their agreement; (2) ask whether their outside CPA or bookkeeper has their own OpenInvoice login. If suppliers routinely add outside accountants as named Users, the fence is where the wedge needs it to be and this turns GREEN for the manual pilot. If Users means employees only, RED for OFS; move to finalist 2 or 3.

**Software path, for later.** Enverus API or partner conversation first. Kill criterion 5 watch item: Enverus is marketing "Enverus ONE, the Governed AI Platform Built for the Energy Industry" and LifeSaver for "Operators, Contractors & Field Crews" (enverus.com, accessed 2026-10-06), so it is moving toward agents and toward the contractor side.
