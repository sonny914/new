# Week one: four solo checks before anyone is called

Nothing here involves another person. Each is one evening or less. Any red box ends the OFS wedge before a dollar or a conversation is spent.

## 1. Portal terms of use

Read, do not search. Open the full documents.

- Enverus OpenInvoice supplier terms (and OpenTicket if separate).
- ServiceChannel provider terms and developer/API terms (for finalist 3, same evening).

Look for language on: third parties acting on a supplier's behalf; automated or scripted access; credential sharing; "authorised user" definitions; prohibitions on agents, bots or screen automation.

| Outcome | Box |
|---|---|
| Third-party operation under written authorisation is permitted or not addressed | GREEN |
| Permitted only through a partner or integrator programme | YELLOW: note the programme, cost and timeline |
| Prohibited, or credential sharing is banned | RED: stop OFS wedge; move to finalist 2 or 3 |

Copy the exact clause text and its URL into this file under the table.

## 2. Buying climate

- WTI price today and 90-day trend.
- Permian and Gulf Coast rig count, current and year over year (Baker Hughes weekly count).
- Three recent headlines on OFS hiring or layoffs in Texas.

| Outcome | Box |
|---|---|
| Rig count flat or up; OFS firms hiring | GREEN |
| Rig count down less than 15% YoY | YELLOW: expect "not now" answers; budget more calls |
| Rig count down more than 15% YoY or layoff headlines dominate | RED: park OFS until the cycle turns |

Record the three numbers and the date.

## 3. What the market already pays for this loop

Pull 20 job postings from the last 90 days (Indeed, LinkedIn, ZipRecruiter, Energy Workforce & Technology Council board) mentioning any of: "OpenInvoice", "field ticket", "ticket to invoice", "oilfield billing", "AR specialist oil and gas".

For each: company, title, salary band if shown, Houston/Permian, and the sentence that describes the chasing part of the job.

| Outcome | Box |
|---|---|
| 10+ postings; salary bands visible; chasing described as part of the job | GREEN: the price anchor is a salary |
| 5–9 postings | YELLOW |
| Under 5 | RED: the loop may be smaller than the vendors claim |

Paste the table into `01-target-list-and-call-script.md` under "Companies hiring for this loop"; those companies are the first names on the target list.

## 4. Target list of fifteen

Build it in `01-target-list-and-call-script.md`. Criteria and sources are there. Do not call anyone this week.

## Day-30 gate (decide now, not later)

All three must hold or the OFS wedge is killed and finalist 2 or 3 takes the same test.

1. One pilot customer agrees in writing to pay at least $1,000/month, or a 0.5% success fee on accelerated receivables, for the manual service to continue.
2. Pending-approval age (submitted to approved) falls at least 20% against that customer's prior 90-day baseline.
3. The pilot ledger shows the same counterparties, reasons and fixes recurring across both pilots.

Nothing is built until a third paying customer exists.
