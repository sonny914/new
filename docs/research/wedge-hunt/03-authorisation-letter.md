# Written authorisation: draft for review by the supplier's counsel and Jay's

**This is a template, not legal advice.** It exists so the two-week pilot has a paper trail, so the supplier's staff know QB is allowed in their systems, and so the portal operator sees an authorised-agent relationship rather than credential sharing. Do not use it until week-one check 1 (portal terms of use) has come back green or yellow, and do not use it with a portal whose terms prohibit the arrangement regardless of what this letter says.

---

**LIMITED AUTHORISATION TO ACT AS BILLING AGENT**

Date: ____________

**Supplier:** ______________________________ ("Supplier"), a ______________ company with its principal office at ______________________________.

**Agent:** Quiet Bands, ______________________________, Houston, Texas ("Agent").

1. **Scope.** Supplier authorises Agent, for the period from ____________ to ____________ (the "Pilot Period"), to perform the following on Supplier's behalf and in Supplier's name, solely in connection with Supplier's accounts receivable from the operators listed in Schedule A:
   a. Access Supplier's accounts on the invoicing portals listed in Schedule A, using credentials issued to Agent by Supplier, to view ticket and invoice status, submit invoices and supporting documents that Supplier has approved, and respond to requests for information.
   b. Communicate with Supplier's customers' field, approval and accounts-payable personnel by email, text message and telephone to obtain ticket signatures, invoice approvals and payment status, identifying itself as acting for Supplier.
   c. Maintain a log of every action taken and every communication made under this authorisation and provide it to Supplier weekly and on request.

2. **Exclusions.** Agent shall not: negotiate, change or waive any price, rate, scope, term or condition of any contract, ticket or invoice; accept any payment or settlement on Supplier's behalf; make any representation about Supplier's work other than what appears on Supplier's own tickets and records; create, alter or back-date any ticket, signature or supporting document; or take any action that Supplier has told Agent in writing not to take. Any matter in these categories is referred to Supplier's designated contact for decision.

3. **Credentials and portal terms.** Supplier represents that it has reviewed the terms of use of each portal in Schedule A and that this authorisation is consistent with them. Supplier will issue Agent its own named user credentials where the portal permits, and will revoke all Agent access at the end of the Pilot Period or on notice. Agent will use the credentials only as described in Section 1 and will not share them.

4. **Confidentiality.** Agent will treat all Supplier and customer information it accesses as confidential, use it only for the purposes of this authorisation, and return or destroy it at the end of the Pilot Period except for the action log, a copy of which Agent may keep with customer and personnel names removed.

5. **Compensation.** No fee is payable for the Pilot Period. Any continuation will be under a separate written agreement.

6. **Liability.** Agent acts on Supplier's instructions and records. Agent is not responsible for the accuracy of Supplier's tickets, pricing or coding, or for any customer's decision to approve, dispute or pay. Each party is responsible for its own acts and omissions. Nothing here makes Agent an employee, partner or joint venturer of Supplier.

7. **Termination.** Either party may end this authorisation at any time on written notice, including email. Sections 2, 4 and 6 survive.

8. **Governing law.** Texas.

## Notes for Jay

- Section 2 is the whole point. It keeps the pilot on the right side of "billing agent" versus "someone negotiating our contracts", and it is the same line the restoration and roofing wedges could not hold.
- Section 3 puts the portal-terms representation on the supplier. If their counsel balks, that is information: the terms probably do not allow it.
- Ask for a named user login, not a shared password. If the portal cannot issue one, log that in the week-one checklist; it is a yellow.
- Keep the signed PDF and the action log together. They are the evidence file if an operator ever asks who was in their portal.
