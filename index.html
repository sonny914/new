Buyer math

We price problems, not software.

Operating baseline→Measurable friction→System intervention→Observed change→Economic effect

In a residential amenity program, that chain runs through eligible residents, the monthly benefit budget, participating vendors, utilization, manual reconciliation hours, disputed records, and the spend nobody can currently measure.

We don't manufacture ROI. Where the data doesn't exist yet, we identify what should start being measured.

If the economics can't support the build, we should know that before anyone writes code.
