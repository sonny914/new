What you buy

Understand the work. Prove the fix. Build the system.

01

Work audit

Find where a high-value process is breaking across people, partners or systems. You get a current-state map, the failure points, the ownership gaps, the evidence, and the numbers that decide whether a build is worth it.

02

Working prototype

Take one high-value break and build the working replacement flow — showing how context, ownership, exceptions and proof actually move through the system.

03

Purpose-built system

Build the real system: intake, eligibility, entitlements, routing, handoffs, staff context, vendor coordination, exception management, reconciliation and reporting.

We price problems, not software. If the economics can't support the build, we should know that before anyone writes code.
