01Run of show

Six steps, one scene.

  1. 01Kill itYour idea goes through the five questions. If it fails, we fix it or swap it before anyone spends a credit.
  2. 02Beat itFive timed beats on paper. The scene exists before a single frame does.
  3. 03Lock itAn identity block and a reference sheet for your lead character.
  4. 04Shoot itA shot list with one prompt per shot. Stills first, then motion.
  5. 05Cut itTrim to the moment, J and L cuts, four layers of sound, captions.
  6. 06Post itA hook in the first frame, one line of context, and the breakdown you’ll post two days later.

02Bring

Three things.

  1. A phone or a laptop. Either works for following along.
  2. One idea. One sentence is enough. It doesn’t have to be good yet; that’s step one.
  3. One image tool and one video tool. Any you already use. Part 12 of the free pack lists some to test.

03Questions
