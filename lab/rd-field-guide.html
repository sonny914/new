The method

Six things we look for, in order.

The Pressure Test asks about each one. The review it produces is rule-based and transparent: no score, no percentage, no AI opinion of your idea.

01ProblemWhat goes wrong, for whom, and how often. A problem that happens rarely rarely deserves software.
02Current behaviorWhat people do today instead. The workaround is the strongest evidence that a problem is real.
03ConsequenceWhat it costs: time, labor, revenue, errors, risk. No consequence, no reason to change.
04EvidenceWhat you have actually seen, as opposed to assumed. Selections are signals, not proof.
05Human validationWhether people doing the work have told you any of this themselves. The next answer usually isn’t online.
06DecisionOne of five outcomes: research more, talk to humans, test manually, explore a build, or park it.

Most ideas that come through here should not be built yet. Quiet Bands builds software for a living, and the review is written so it can still say so.

Only ideas with observable evidence, a real workaround, a real cost and real conversations are pointed toward a project conversation. Everything else gets a next experiment instead.
