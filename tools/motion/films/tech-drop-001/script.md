## What changed from the working script, and why

- **"which they say generates outputs up to 30 percent faster than 5.1" → "Up to thirty percent faster than 5.1."** The "up to" and the "vs 5.1" stay in the voice. "Output speed · as announced" is on screen. There's no comparison to Opus anywhere.
- **"Missing a dependency? It can identify it." → "It can find it."** Same claim, easier to say.
- **"Need network access? It'll ask for approval." → "It asks for your approval."** The approval belongs to the user, which the brief requires.
- **"You can enter it securely without putting it in the conversation" → "You enter it securely, outside the chat."** Same claim, half the length.
- **The two slash commands are now named and explained.** /usage diagnoses and offers a fix; /storage removes unused worktrees and sessions. The working script only said "see what's eating up your usage" and "clean up old worktrees".
- **"They also brought back ultrathink for those problems that need extra reasoning" → "Ultrathink is back. Extra reasoning, for one turn."** The single-turn scope is in the brief's facts.
- **"once that environment is configured, you can come back from another device" → "Your session keeps running in the cloud. Pick it up from another device."** It now carries both facts: the session runs without your machine, and you continue elsewhere.
- **Order:** the brief's sequences run upgrade, cloud, storage/usage, ultrathink/models, devices. The slash commands therefore come before ultrathink, and "another device" moves to the final sequence.
- **"Because the real shift isn't just smarter AI. It's AI that can handle more of the work…" → "The real shift? AI handling more of the work between the idea and the finished product."** It's one line instead of two, for the runtime.
