# Film 003: THE MONEY CHASE. Final narration (Jay / Flighty voice)

**The argument:** chasing money without understanding the exchange that produces it gives you endless activity and no progress. This isn't an anti-money film. The bills are real. The line it draws is between wanting income and mistaking constant activity for a way to earn it.

**Arc:** urgency, then temptation, acceleration, exhaustion, revelation and clarity.

Each line is its own take in the authorized Flighty voice (Higgsfield `seed_audio`, custom voice element). The takes are sequenced with designed pauses. The picture, captions and sound are all timed to the real audio. Nothing was sped up. The runtime is 74.9 s.

## Changes from the working script

- **The order of the middle section.** "We get so focused on how to GET the money, we stop asking why somebody would GIVE it to us" now comes after "attention ain't a business model". The emotional arc you asked for runs from exhaustion to revelation. The $0 moment has to come before the realization that money moves through exchanges. "But here's where we mess up" now leads straight into the ten apps. That keeps the turn, and the GET/GIVE line becomes the setup for the reveal.
- **"Everybody chasing money" became "Everybody's chasing money."** Seed voice renders the bare form as a stumble. It's the same sound in Jay's register, just cleaner.
- **"the next thing that's supposed to make 'em rich" became "make you rich".** It speaks to the viewer, who is the person chasing.
- **"why somebody would GIVE it to us" became "why anybody would give it to us".** It's wider, and it's a common Jay construction.
- **"exchange something for it" became "trade something for it".** It's easier to say and lands harder.
- **The four questions** stay as quotable prompts ("How they're solving it right now" adds "right now" for rhythm).
- No slang was added. The two "ain't"s are from the working script.
