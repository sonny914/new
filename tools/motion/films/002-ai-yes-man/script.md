# Film 002: THE AI YES MAN. Final narration (Jay / Flighty voice)

The argument is that AI encouragement is not evidence of demand, so use it to challenge your thinking, not confirm it.

The revision opens on the hook, keeps the four locked lines and keeps all four challenge questions in a form people can copy into their own assistant. The jokes come from the contrast, not from punchlines. Each line is generated as its own take and sequenced with designed pauses; the picture is timed to the real audio.

| # | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | AI will have you thinking you're cooking. | The hook. Amused, knowing, no wind-up |
| 02 | You tell it your business idea… and suddenly everything's brilliant. | A beat after "idea". Mock-impressed on "brilliant" |

About 125 words, targeting 52–58 seconds with pauses.

## Changes from the working script

- **Opening:** "You know what I think one of the biggest problems with AI is? It'll let you think you're cooking." became the hook you preferred, "AI will have you thinking you're cooking." The question intro slows the first two seconds.
- **"See, AI can help you build an idea" became "Look, AI can help you build an idea."** "Look" reads as a turn to fairness without sounding like a lesson.
- **"Because the goal isn't to have a machine tell you you're right" became "The point isn't getting a machine to tell you you're right."** It's shorter and lands better out loud.
- **Locked lines kept:** "you're cooking", "you ain't sold a damn thing", "agreement ain't validation", and "Make the strongest argument against my plan". All four questions are kept word for word.
