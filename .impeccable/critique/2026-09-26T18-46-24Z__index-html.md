## Design specificity verdict

**Design review.** Half authored. The idea is Quiet Bands' and nobody else's: a site map that is a broken object, four shards that are the nav, an R&D page that says "one shipped product is listed because one has shipped." The settled frame and /rd/ could not be lifted by another studio without looking stolen. But the frame most visitors will judge the studio on, the rest frame, is a whole, pristine wireframe bulb with blueprint marks on near-black: the reference, and a hundred dark engineering-studio heroes. The hairline at rest is invisible at 1x. The share image, with the cracks fully drawn, is the more Quiet Bands frame than the homepage's own first frame. Nothing on the entry says software or Houston; the one sentence that carries the thesis is 15 px at 55% alpha in a corner.

Synthesis note: the brief pins "whole bulb on deep black" as step 1 and the client asked for the cracks to develop with the scroll, so the fix here is bounded: a hairline that is legible at 1x on a phone, on the face toward the viewer, not a cracked rest frame.

## Overall impression

The mechanism is the message and it works: the break is the peak, everything is a pure function of scroll, orange is spent on exactly one thing at a time. What it lacks is an invitation and a way back. The page never asks to be scrolled, hides its navigation from keyboards and screen readers until the last tenth of the scroll, dims its one conversion behind fog, and ends on a text page with no route home. The single biggest opportunity is the first fifth of the scroll: reward the first flick and the whole sequence reads as intended.

## What's working

- **The fragment is the nav, not a button.** Hover or focus turns every line of the shard orange and eases it forward; the label is a bare word beside it. Orange as material, not as UI.
- **Everything is a function of t.** Scrubbing back rebuilds the bulb; there is no state to get wrong. The metaphor works mechanically as well as visually.
- **The /rd/ voice.** "One shipped product is listed because one has shipped." "No one has watched a stranger use it." More specific to Quiet Bands than any pixel of the entry.

## Priority issues

- **[P1] No affordance to scroll and a dead first fifth.** At rest nothing moves and nothing hints; the hold to 0.18 means the first flick barely changes anything. The entire site sits behind a scroll the page never asks for. Fix: start the cracks growing from the first pixel (hold ≈ 0.05) so the first scroll is rewarded, and let the sentence do the asking: make it the last thing to fade so its fade is the cue. Suggested command: $impeccable clarify.
- **[P1] Keyboard and screen-reader visitors have no navigation until t≈0.86, and reduced-motion visitors never see the name.** The four links carry `aria-hidden="true"` and `tabindex="-1"` until visible; a screen reader gets a heading, a sentence and nothing. The reduced-motion still hides the wordmark and the line. Fix: keep the links in the accessibility tree and focusable always; on focus, scroll the track to its end so the shard is on screen; show the mark in reduced motion. Suggested command: $impeccable harden.
- **[P1] Contact is the least visible thing on the page.** It is the smallest fragment, placed at the far depth inside the fog band, so it renders at about a third of the intensity of Experiments, and the studio's one conversion is two hops from a mailto at the bottom of everything. Depth hierarchy is accidental (fragment geometry), not authored. Fix: give Contact a near depth, or exempt settled shards from fog; put the quiet contact line on the entry itself. Suggested command: $impeccable layout.
- **[P2] Debris reads as a starfield at rest and as per-dot confetti when tilted.** Seeds fly outward with no bound, so at t=1 points fill the corners far from any shard; hue is random per point, so off-axis it is not one foil surface. The brief bans a starfield and asks for iridescence that reads as one sheet. Fix: fade alpha with distance from the bulb's volume; derive hue from position so neighbours share it; fewer points at rest. Suggested command: $impeccable quieter.
- **[P2] Arrival on /rd/ is a jump cut with no way back, and its outline skips a level.** The header is not sticky, the current-section mark is invisible, the header wraps on a phone, `#contact` can never become current, and the page goes h1 to h3 (the detector's finding). Fix: a slim sticky row with the wordmark; mark current with orange or weight; promote the Lab 001 sub-heads to h2 or add an h2 above them. Suggested command: $impeccable polish.
