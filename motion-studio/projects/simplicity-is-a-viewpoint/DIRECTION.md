# Direction — Simplicity is a viewpoint

Creative decisions for the 15-second showcase, and what was rejected. Scored 1–5 on the studio's criteria: **Cl**arity, **Or**iginality, **Br**and alignment, **Fe**asibility, **Re**usability, **Mo**tion quality, render **Pe**rformance.

## The idea

The film argues that simplicity is a point of view, so the point of view is literally a point: one orange accent (the brand's single live colour) moves through an overloaded system, removes what is not needed, and becomes the full stop of the sentence. The word COMPLEXITY is never replaced; it *changes*. COMPLEXITY and SIMPLICITY share six letters in the same positions (M P L I T Y), so only C O E X roll through their slots. The message is carried by the mechanics.

## Treatments considered

| Treatment | Cl | Or | Br | Fe | Re | Mo | Pe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. One persistent word that changes letter by letter; the accent clears the clutter and lands as the full stop** | 5 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **Chosen.** Every beat is caused by something on screen. |
| B. Separate title cards per scene (COMPLEXITY, then a cut to SIMPLICITY IS A VIEWPOINT.) | 5 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 5 | Rejected: disconnected cards, the brief's explicit "avoid". |
| C. Logo assembled from its three parts (bowls + stem flying in) | 3 | 3 | 1 | 4 | 2 | 4 | 5 | Rejected: the identity rules forbid splitting the mark. The mark opens whole through an iris instead. |
| D. The orange element expands to a full orange field for the reveal | 4 | 3 | 1 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 5 | Rejected: orange is the one live accent, never a ground; the ground is black. |
| E. Particle burst when the clutter clears | 2 | 1 | 1 | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | Rejected: banned decoration; clearing is done by un-drawing what was drawn. |
| F. 3D camera fly-through of the clutter | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 1 | Rejected: no meaning gained; a 2D push/pull on one group says "pressure" and "release" for free. |

## Decisions that came from looking at renders

- **Swap mechanics.** The first swap overlapped old and new letters mid-roll (they were on different curves). Changed letters now roll as glued pairs, the old exactly one slot above the new, on the in-out curve, so it reads as an odometer and never as a smear.
- **Clearing window.** Elements far from the rule were clearing when the accent later landed elsewhere. The sweep only counts the pass (`window.to`).
- **Pacing.** The first render held completely still for 1.5 s in scene 1 and again after the clearing. The clutter now starts earlier and the pass is slower, so the holds that remain are the ones that mean something: the freeze, the clarity beat, the sentence, the end card.
- **Opening frame.** The lattice pre-rolls (`"start-8"`), so frame 0 already shows the system forming.
- **Sound.** The riser cuts to digital silence on the freeze frame; the full stop gets one tick. The bed is normalised to −1.5 dBFS peak (about −19 LUFS) so it is audible on a phone without competing with music added at publish.
